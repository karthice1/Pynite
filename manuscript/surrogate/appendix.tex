\section{Feature dictionary}\label{app:features}
Table~\ref{tab:features} lists the node and member features of the proposed network. Notation: node $k$ at floor level $l$ (0 at the base) on column line $c$; $sb=l-1$ and $sa=l$ are the storeys below and above the node; $s_\mathrm{ref}=sa$ if $l<n_s$ and $sb$ otherwise; $\lambda_\mathrm{W}$, $\lambda_\mathrm{G}$, $H$, $I_\mathrm{ref}$ as in Section~\ref{sec:scaling}; $u_0=\lambda_\mathrm{W}H^3/(EI_\mathrm{ref})$. A feature that refers to a missing neighbour (e.g.\ the column below a base node) is set to zero. For the combined-case ablation, $\lambda_\mathrm{W}$ and $\lambda_\mathrm{G}$ are both replaced by $|\lambda_\mathrm{W}|+\lambda_\mathrm{G}$.

\section{Sampling procedure}\label{app:sampling}
Each frame of the ID, OOD-G and OOD-L sets is drawn with its own seed by the following steps (\texttt{frames.sample\_frame}); the DES frames are generated as described in \ref{app:des}.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Topology. ID and OOD-L: $n_s\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,5\}$, $n_b\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,4\}$. OOD-G: with probability 0.5, $n_s\sim\mathcal{U}\{6,7,8\}$ and $n_b\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,6\}$; otherwise $n_s\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,8\}$ and $n_b\sim\mathcal{U}\{5,6\}$.
\item Geometry. $L_b\sim\mathcal{U}(3,9)$ per bay and $h_s\sim\mathcal{U}(2.8,4.5)$ per storey; OOD-G replaces $h_1$ by $\mathcal{U}(4.5,6.0)$ with probability 0.5.
\item Material and sections. $E\sim\log\mathcal{U}(2.5\times10^7,2.1\times10^8)$; base column $I_{c,1}\sim\log\mathcal{U}(5\times10^{-5},2\times10^{-3})$ with $I_{c,s}=I_{c,1}t^{(s-1)/\max(n_s-1,1)}$, $t\sim\mathcal{U}(0.5,1)$; $I_{b,s}\sim\log\mathcal{U}(5\times10^{-5},2\times10^{-3})$ independently per storey; $r_c,r_b\sim\mathcal{U}(0.08,0.30)$ and $A=I/r^2$.
\item Supports. Fixed bases with probability 0.7, pinned otherwise.
\item Gravity load. $w_s\sim\mathcal{U}(5,40)$ per floor on all bays. OOD-L: each bay keeps $w_s$ with probability 0.5 and otherwise only a dead part $d_s w_s$, $d_s\sim\mathcal{U}(0.3,0.6)$.
\item Lateral load. Roof force $F_\mathrm{roof}\sim\mathcal{U}(5,80)$; profile uniform or inverted triangular (ID, OOD-G) or parabolic or roof-only (OOD-L), each with equal probability; the sign of all floor forces is reversed with probability 0.5.
\item Analysis. PyNite linear analysis of load cases W and G; the statics check of Section~\ref{sec:data} must pass.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Design-informed synthetic frames (DES)}\label{app:des}
Each of the 300 DES frames is generated with its own seed ($9\times10^6+k$, $k=0,\dots,299$; \texttt{frames.sample\_design\_frame}, called by \texttt{experiment\_validation.py}) as follows. Units are kN and m; $\mathcal{U}$ denotes a uniform distribution.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Topology. $n_s\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,5\}$, $n_b\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,4\}$ (realised counts for 1--5 storeys: 58, 62, 64, 50, 66; for 1--4 bays: 74, 76, 73, 77).
\item Geometry. Each bay width is drawn from $\{6.0, 7.5, 9.0\}$ with equal probability; all storeys above the first have the same height $h\sim\mathcal{U}(3.2,3.8)$ and the first storey $h_1\sim\mathcal{U}(4.0,4.5)$.
\item Gravity load. Tributary width $b\sim\mathcal{U}(4,6)$, dead load $D\sim\mathcal{U}(3.5,5.0)$\,kPa, live load $L_q\sim\mathcal{U}(2.0,3.0)$\,kPa. Floor line load $w=(1.2D+1.0L_q)\,b$ on every bay; roof line load $w_\mathrm{roof}=(1.2(D-1.0)+1.0\cdot1.0)\,b$.
\item Lateral load. Wind pressure $q_w\sim\mathcal{U}(0.6,1.2)$\,kPa; the force at floor $s$ is $F_s=q_w\,b\,(h_s+h_{s+1})/2$ with $h_{n_s+1}=0$; all forces change sign with probability 0.5.
\item Beam sizing (one section for all floors). With $L_{\max}$ the largest bay, $I_\mathrm{defl}=5L_q b L_{\max}^4/(384E\,L_{\max}/360)$ (live-load deflection $L/360$) and $I_\mathrm{str}=0.15\,(w_{\max}L_{\max}^2/8)/f_y$ with $f_y=250$\,MPa (simple-span moment, section depth about 0.3\,m); $I_b=\max(I_\mathrm{defl},I_\mathrm{str})$.
\item Column sizing. Storey shear $V_s=\sum_{k\ge s}|F_k|$; $I_{c,s}=V_s h_s^3/(0.5\cdot12E(n_b+1)\,h_s/400)$, i.e.\ the shear-building drift equals $h_s/400$ with the column stiffness halved for beam flexibility; within each pair of storeys $(1,2),(3,4),\dots$ the larger value is used for both; finally $I_{c,s}\ge0.3I_b$.
\item Clipping. $I_c$ and $I_b$ are clipped to $[5\times10^{-5}, 2\times10^{-3}]$\,m$^4$, the training range (28\% of the frames have at least one clipped column section, 0.3\% a clipped beam section). Areas follow from radii of gyration $r_c=0.16$\,m and $r_b=0.18$\,m, $A=I/r^2$.
\item Supports. Fixed bases with probability 0.8, pinned otherwise.
\item Analysis. PyNite linear analysis of cases W and G; the statics check of Section~\ref{sec:data} must pass. No frame is rejected.
\end{enumerate}
