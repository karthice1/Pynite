\section{Feature dictionary}\label{app:features}
Table~\ref{tab:features} lists the node and member features of the proposed network. Notation: node $k$ at floor level $l$ (0 at the base) on column line $c$; $sb=l-1$ and $sa=l$ are the storeys below and above the node; $s_\mathrm{ref}=sa$ if $l<n_s$ and $sb$ otherwise; $\lambda_\mathrm{W}$, $\lambda_\mathrm{G}$, $H$, $I_\mathrm{ref}$ as in Section~\ref{sec:scaling}; $u_0=\lambda_\mathrm{W}H^3/(EI_\mathrm{ref})$. A feature that refers to a missing neighbour (e.g.\ the column below a base node) is set to zero. For the combined-case ablation, $\lambda_\mathrm{W}$ and $\lambda_\mathrm{G}$ are both replaced by $|\lambda_\mathrm{W}|+\lambda_\mathrm{G}$.

\section{Sampling procedure}\label{app:sampling}
Each frame of the ID, OOD-G and OOD-L sets is drawn with its own seed by the following steps (\texttt{frames.sample\_frame}); the DES frames follow Section~\ref{sec:data} (\texttt{frames.sample\_design\_frame}).
\begin{enumerate}
\item Topology. ID and OOD-L: $n_s\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,5\}$, $n_b\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,4\}$. OOD-G: with probability 0.5, $n_s\sim\mathcal{U}\{6,7,8\}$ and $n_b\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,6\}$; otherwise $n_s\sim\mathcal{U}\{1,\dots,8\}$ and $n_b\sim\mathcal{U}\{5,6\}$.
\item Geometry. $L_b\sim\mathcal{U}(3,9)$ per bay and $h_s\sim\mathcal{U}(2.8,4.5)$ per storey; OOD-G replaces $h_1$ by $\mathcal{U}(4.5,6.0)$ with probability 0.5.
\item Material and sections. $E\sim\log\mathcal{U}(2.5\times10^7,2.1\times10^8)$; base column $I_{c,1}\sim\log\mathcal{U}(5\times10^{-5},2\times10^{-3})$ with $I_{c,s}=I_{c,1}t^{(s-1)/\max(n_s-1,1)}$, $t\sim\mathcal{U}(0.5,1)$; $I_{b,s}\sim\log\mathcal{U}(5\times10^{-5},2\times10^{-3})$ independently per storey; $r_c,r_b\sim\mathcal{U}(0.08,0.30)$ and $A=I/r^2$.
\item Supports. Fixed bases with probability 0.7, pinned otherwise.
\item Gravity load. $w_s\sim\mathcal{U}(5,40)$ per floor on all bays. OOD-L: each bay keeps $w_s$ with probability 0.5 and otherwise only a dead part $d_s w_s$, $d_s\sim\mathcal{U}(0.3,0.6)$.
\item Lateral load. Roof force $F_\mathrm{roof}\sim\mathcal{U}(5,80)$; profile uniform or inverted triangular (ID, OOD-G) or parabolic or roof-only (OOD-L), each with equal probability; the sign of all floor forces is reversed with probability 0.5.
\item Analysis. PyNite linear analysis of load cases W and G; the statics check of Section~\ref{sec:data} must pass.
\end{enumerate}
