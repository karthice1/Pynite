The batched surrogate is 12--14 times faster than PyNite per frame, but an optimised sparse stiffness solver and OpenSeesPy analyse the same frames 1.5--3 times faster than the surrogate. For cheap first-order linear analyses the speed argument therefore does not hold; it was the main justification in the original formulation of this study and has been revised. The accuracy, admissibility and referral results do not depend on it, and they are the parts of the framework that carry over to expensive analyses.
